# Plansze grafik

Metoda jak w Zakupach: jeden kwadrat, 8 × 8 pól, tło #0A1626, kolejność pól = kolejność cięcia.
Miniatury części i narzędzi to ilustracje poglądowe: w aplikacji muszą mieć taki podpis i nigdy nie zastępują numeru części.

## Plansza 1: części i narzędzia
```
Create one single square image, as large as possible (at least 2048×2048 px), showing a strict grid of exactly 8 columns and 8 rows, 64 equal square cells in total.

Style: photorealistic studio product photography of generic car parts and workshop tools, consistent soft lighting from the top left, each item centered and filling about 75% of its cell, front or slight 3/4 view, realistic materials.

Background: the entire image is one flat solid dark navy colour #0A1626, with no gradient, no shadows on the background, no reflections, no grid lines, no borders and no gaps of a different colour.

Strictly forbidden: any text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names, labels, price tags or watermarks. Packaging must be plain and unbranded.

Cells, left to right, top to bottom:
Row 1: black interior car door pull handle; black exterior car door handle; metal exterior door handle carrier mechanism; plastic door panel trim clips; convex car side mirror glass; complete black car side mirror; car window regulator with cables and rails; small black plastic window glass clip.
Row 2: small plastic bushing; roll of black butyl sealant tape; compact flat electric window motor; single hex bolt; car door lock actuator with connector; black rubber door seal strip coiled; small plastic sill trim clips; long black plastic door sill trim strip.
Row 3: black inner window felt seal strip; black outer window seal strip; set of plastic trim removal tools; T20 Torx screwdriver; 10 mm socket on a small ratchet; roll of blue masking tape; flathead and Phillips screwdrivers crossed; ball of string.
Row 4: black cable ties bundle; utility knife; U-shaped trim clip removal fork; hex Allen key set; telescopic magnetic pick-up tool; wide flat plastic wedge; folded microfiber cloth; digital multimeter.
Row 5: combination pliers; flat metal pry spatula; rubber mallet; plain spray bottle of soapy water; plain metal can of solvent; plain work gloves; safety glasses; headlamp flashlight.
Row 6: hydraulic trolley jack; pair of axle stands; pair of yellow wheel chocks; click torque wrench; breaker bar with socket; plain car battery; battery terminal wrench; car battery charger without display text.
Row 7: pair of brake pads; ventilated brake disc; brake caliper; plain oil filter; air filter panel; cabin pollen filter; fuel filter canister; set of wiper blades.
Row 8: glow plug; serpentine ribbed belt; water pump; radiator hose; control arm with ball joint; shock absorber; wheel bearing hub; plain oil canister.

Every cell must contain exactly one subject. Do not skip, merge or reorder cells.
```
